Setting up on Meta (free)

  1. At developers.facebook.com, create an app of type Business and add the WhatsApp product.
  2. WhatsApp > API Setup gives you a free test number. Add up to five phones under "To" to try it with.
  3. Copy the Phone Number ID and a token into step 2 above, and the App secret from App settings > Basic. Tick "Turn WhatsApp on" and save.
  4. Put the Callback URL and Verify token from step 1 into Meta, and subscribe to messages.
  5. On a test phone, open WhatsApp in this app's menu, get a code, and send it. Then ask cash balance.
  6. To go live: add your own business number in Meta, create a permanent System User token, and complete Meta business verification. Update step 2 with the new number's details.
